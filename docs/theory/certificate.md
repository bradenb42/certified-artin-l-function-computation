# The certificate

**Purpose.** A record $\mathcal{C}(f,X)$ from which a verifier $\mathsf{V}$, given only $f$, $X$ and $\mathcal{C}$, decides by recomputation whether every output of the pipeline is correct, without repeating any search: no descent, no subgroup enumeration, no set cover, no splitting-field construction, no Brauer decomposition is redone; each is *presented* and *checked*. Every item below is a triple (content, check, justification), the justification being the theorem that makes the check sufficient. Throughout, "at precision $k$" means the verifier recomputes modulo $\ell^{k}$ (in the given tower for ramified $\ell$) and compares exactly.

## 0. Global part

**G0.1 Input and numbering.** $f$; the numbering prime $p\nmid M\cdot\mathrm{disc}f$ ([cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) §6); the roots $\hat\alpha\subset\mathcal{O}\_q/p^{k\_p}$. *Check:* $f(\hat\alpha\_i)\equiv0$, the $\hat\alpha\_i$ distinct modulo $p$, $k\_p$ as in G0.4. *Justification:* [the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) §5 (Hensel lifting is unique from simple residue roots).

**G0.2 Galois group and descent certificate.** Generators of $G\le S\_n$ with a BSGS; the chain $S\_n=G\_0>\dots>G\_t=G$, transversals $T\_i$, invariants $F\_i$ (with Tschirnhaus transforms), the integers $c\_i$, and the exact resolvents $\mathrm{Res}*i=\prod*{\tau\in G\_{i-1}/G\_i}(x-F\_i(\tau\alpha))\in\mathbb{Z}[x]$ with $\mathrm{disc},\mathrm{Res}*i\ne0$. Check: $\mathrm{Stab}*{G\_{i-1}}(F\_i)=G\_i$ (permutation arithmetic); $\mathrm{Res}\_i$ reproduced from $\hat\alpha$ at precision $k\_p$ and $\mathrm{disc},\mathrm{Res}*i\ne0$; $F\_i(\hat\alpha)\equiv c\_i\bmod p^{k\_p}$ with $|c\_i|\le B*{F\_i}$; $\mathrm{Res}\_i(c\_i)=0$. *Justification:* [the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) Prop. 4.1 and Theorem 5.1 (T1, T2): these congruences at precision $k\_p$ prove $F\_i(\alpha)=c\_i$ and the distinctness, hence $\mathrm{Gal}\le G\_i$ at each level and $\mathrm{Gal}=G$ once $G$ is also shown to be contained (the descent's lower bound: the certified Frobenius classes of §2 generate $G$, or the recorded $\mathrm{Res}$ irreducibility data; the pipeline records a set of certified Frobenius classes whose generated subgroup is $G$).

**G0.3 Character table.** The table $(\chi(g\_k))$ in $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta\_e]$, class representatives $g\_k$, power maps, the set $S$ and the exact class matrices $M\_i$, $i\in S$; $\mathbb{Q}(\chi)$, $\nu\_2(\chi)$, $\det\rho\_\chi$, Schur index data. *Check:* [the character table](https://claude.ai/chat/character-table.md) Prop. 2.2 (exact eigenvector identities and distinct tuples), orthogonality, $\chi(1)\mid|G|$, $\det\rho\_\chi$ by [the global conductor](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md) Prop. 2.1. *Justification:* [the character table](https://claude.ai/chat/character-table.md) Prop. 2.2.

**G0.4 Precision policy.** $R$, $\Delta=|\mathrm{disc}f|$, the list $\mathcal{F}$ of pairs $(F,m)$ with $(\deg F,|F|*1)$, $M^\*$, $\mathbf{M}$, and $k*\ell=\lfloor\log\_\ell\mathbf{M}\rfloor+1$ for every prime used. *Check:* recompute $\mathbf{M}$ from the listed pairs and confirm every invariant used anywhere in $\mathcal{C}$ belongs to $\mathcal{F}$ with the recorded $m$. *Justification:* [the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) Theorem 5.1.

**G0.5 Ramified primes.** The factorizations of the $\mathrm{disc}(f\_j)$; for each candidate $\ell$ the verdict with witness: odd valuation, or Dedekind's criterion data, or the $\ell$-maximal order $\mathcal{O}'$ with its radical $I\_\ell(\mathcal{O}')$ and the equality $[I\_\ell\:I\_\ell]=\mathcal{O}'$; the invariants $(e\_j,f\_j)$ and $v\_\ell(\mathrm{disc},\mathcal{O}*{K\_j})$. Check: recompute the radical and the multiplier ring once (*[*the ramified primes*](https://claude.ai/chat/ramified-primes.md) *Thm. 2.2) and $I*\ell(\mathcal{O}')=\ell\mathcal{O}'$ or not. *Justification:* [the ramified primes](https://claude.ai/chat/ramified-primes.md) Theorem 1.1, Prop. 2.1, Theorem 2.2.

**G0.6 Abelian data.** $c$, $U$, the pinning of $\iota$ ([cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Prop. 1.3: the periods basis, the coefficients $b\_i$, the matched $g\in G^{ab}$ per $x$), $M$, $G'$. *Check:* recompute the period identities at precision $k\_p$; $\iota(p)=\pi(\mathrm{Frob}\_p)$ with $\mathrm{Frob}\_p$ from $\hat\alpha^p$. *Justification:* [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Prop. 1.3 to 1.4.

## 1. Per ramified prime $\ell$

**R1 Local factorization and tower (**[**local Galois groups**](https://claude.ai/chat/local-galois-groups.md)**).** $f\bmod\ell^{\nu+1}$ factored as $\prod g\_j$ with the resultant valuations $v\_\ell(\mathrm{Res}(g\_a,g\_b))<(\nu+1)/2$; the tower $L^{(1)}\subset\dots\subset L=L^{(s)}$ with, at each step, the adjoined polynomial $q$ and its degree, the uniformizer and residue generator of each field; the roots $\beta\_1,\dots,\beta\_n$ as tower elements at precision $k\_\ell$; for each generator $d\_k$ of $D$ its images of the tower generators; the orders $|M\_j|$, $|D|$, $f(L)$, $e(L)$. *Check:* the factorization congruence and the resultant condition; irreducibility of each $q$ over its base by [the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) Lemma 3.3 (the recorded field data at precision $>v(\mathrm{disc},q)$); each $d\_k$ is an automorphism: its images are roots of the transported minimal polynomials and it permutes ${\beta\_i}$ as the recorded permutation; the group generated has order $|D|$ and is solvable; orbits on ${\beta\_i}$ are the root sets of the $g\_j$. *Justification:* [local Galois groups](https://claude.ai/chat/local-galois-groups.md) Prop. 1.1, Prop. 2.2, Theorem 4.1 (the generated group is contained in $\mathrm{Gal}(L/\mathbb{Q}\_\ell)$ and has the right order).

**R2 Matching $\tau$ (**[**matching**](https://claude.ai/chat/matching.md) **to** [**well-definedness**](https://claude.ai/chat/well-definedness.md)**).** The permutation $\rho$; for each level $i$ the selected $\tau'*i\in T\_i$ and the residue $F\_i(\beta^{\pi\_i\tau'i})\bmod\ell^{k\ell}$ that matched $c\_i$; the filtered sets $C\_i$ with the structural-filter reason. Check: $F\_i(\beta^\rho)\equiv c\_i\pmod{\ell^{k*\ell}}$ for all $i$ (evaluation in the tower); $\rho^{-1}d\_k\rho\in G$ for every generator $d\_k$ (sifting). *Justification:* [matching](https://claude.ai/chat/matching.md) Theorem 2.3 (these two checks are exactly the certificate of a matching); [well-definedness](https://claude.ai/chat/well-definedness.md) Theorem 3.3 (the outputs below are invariant under the ambiguity $G\rho$).

**R3 Filtration (**[**the filtration**](https://claude.ai/chat/ramification-filtration.md) **to** [**filtration uniqueness**](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md)**).** $D\_\ell=\rho^{-1}D\rho$, $I\_\ell$, $\Phi$ (with $\omega$), the class-of-$G$ labels of all elements of $D\_\ell$ ([Euler factors](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md) §4), the uniformizer $\varpi\_L$ and, for one representative $\sigma$ of each $D$-class inside $I$, the valuation $i\_L(\sigma)=v\_L(\sigma\varpi\_L-\varpi\_L)$; the chain $(D\_i)$ with orders, $\varphi\_L$, the lower and upper breaks; $\delta(L)$ and the independent $v\_L(m'(\varpi\_L))$; the Hasse to Arf table (upper jumps of every abelian $D\_a/D\_b$); when [filtration uniqueness](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md) §2 was used: $S$, $y\_S$, $R\_S$, its Newton polygon and the labeled valuations $v\_L(\tau y\_S-y\_S)$. *Check:* $\omega$ Teichmüller and $\sigma\omega=\omega$ characterizes $I$ ([the filtration](https://claude.ai/chat/ramification-filtration.md) Prop. 1.1); recompute each recorded $i\_L(\sigma)$ at precision $\nu+1$; $D\_i={i\_L\ge i+1}$ normal with elementary abelian quotients; Hilbert $\sum\_{\sigma\ne1}i\_L(\sigma)=v\_L(m'(\varpi\_L))$; Hasse to Arf integrality; orbit sizes of $I,P$ on each $g\_j$ against $(e\_j,f\_j)$ and $\ell^{a\_j}$ ([filtration uniqueness](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md) Check 2); $\varphi\_L$ and the upper breaks recomputed from the orders; if $R\_S$ is present, $y\_S$ generates $\mathcal{O}\_{L^S}$ ([the ramified primes](https://claude.ai/chat/ramified-primes.md) test), the polygon slopes equal $-(|S\cap I|+i\_L(\sigma)-1)$ on wild cosets ([filtration uniqueness](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md) Thm. 2.2). *Justification:* [the filtration](https://claude.ai/chat/ramification-filtration.md) Prop. 2.2, Lemma 2.3, Theorem 4.2; [filtration uniqueness](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md) Theorems 1.3, 2.2, §3.

**R4 Local root-number data.** Tame $\ell$: the good uniformizer with $\varpi\_L^e=\omega\ell$, $\iota$, the characters $\theta\_j$ with $n\_{\theta\_j}$, per orbit the elements $\Phi\_\theta$ with the correction $\sigma$, $\eta\_\Phi$, $r$, the Gauss sums and the determinants $\det(\rho\_\chi(\Phi\_\theta)|V\_\theta)$ (as products of extension-character values) for every $\chi$. Wild $\ell$: for every $\chi$ the decomposition $\theta=\sum c\_{A,\mu}\mathrm{Ind}*A^D(\mu-\mathbf{1})$ (subgroups $A$ by generators, $\mu$ on $A^{ab}$, coefficients), the conductor exponents $a(\xi*{A,\mu})$ and $d\_A$, the Lubin to Tate data (the torsion field, the embedding of $M$, the values $\xi\_{A,\mu}$ on a generating set of $(\mathcal{O}/\mathfrak{p}^a)^\times$ and at $\pi$), and the resulting $\varepsilon(\xi\_{A,\mu},\psi\_{F\_A})$. *Check:* tame: $\varpi\_L^e=\omega\ell$; $\theta\_0(\sigma)=\sigma\varpi\_L/\varpi\_L$; $n\_\theta=\langle\chi|*I,\theta\rangle$ from the table; $\Phi*\theta(\varpi\_L^{e/m'})=\varpi\_L^{e/m'}r^{q-1}$ and Frobenius on residues; Gauss sums recomputed; $|\varepsilon\_\ell(\chi)|=1$ and [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md) §4 checks. Wild: the decomposition identity as class functions on $D$ (values on class representatives); $a(\xi)$ and $d\_A$ from the filtration by [conductor exponents](https://claude.ai/chat/conductor-exponents.md) Thm. 2.1 and Prop. 2.2; the reciprocity values reproduced by evaluating $[u^{-1}]$ on the recorded torsion point and matching against $\mathrm{Gal}(M/F\_A)$ ([wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) Prop. 2.3); Tate sums recomputed; $|\varepsilon|=1$; [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) §3 checks (ii) to (v). *Justification:* [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md) Theorems 2.3, 3.1; [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) Theorems 1.1, 3.1.

## 2. Per unramified prime $\ell\le X$

**U1 Block.** The cycle type $\lambda$ of $f\bmod\ell$. *Check:* factor $f\bmod\ell$. Trivial.

**U2 Rational class.** For each $H\in\mathcal{H}*\lambda$ (the separating family of* [*separating subgroups*](https://claude.ai/chat/separating-subgroups.md) *for the block), the count $\mathrm{fix}*{G/H}(\mathrm{Frob}*\ell)$ and, if $\ell\in E\_H$, the $\ell$-adic root count of $R\_H$ at precision $k*\ell$; the resulting rational class. *Check:* factor $R\_H\bmod\ell$ (or count $\ell$-adically) and compare with $\pi\_H$ evaluated on the claimed rational class ([rational classes](https://claude.ai/chat/rational-classes.md) Lemma 1.1, from the table); confirm that the family's signatures are distinct on the block (a table computation, done once and referenced). *Justification:* [rational classes](https://claude.ai/chat/rational-classes.md) Theorem 4.1, [separating subgroups](https://claude.ai/chat/separating-subgroups.md) Theorems 1.3(4), 3.3.

**U3 Conjugacy class, when finer than the rational class.** Either ([cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md)) $t=\ell\bmod M$, the $G'$-rational-class data, and for each twisted resolvent $R\_{C,\chi}$ used the prime $\lambda$ of $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta\_o]$ (by its defining factor), the counts $n\_j$, and the deduced class of $\sigma\_\ell^{f}$, together with the residue class of $\ell$ in the exactness set of [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Thm. 5.1; or ([the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md)) the alignment $\pi$ with the residues $F\_i(\beta^{\pi\tau'*i})\bmod\ell$ that matched $c\_i$ and the Frobenius permutation $\phi*\beta$, with $\beta$ not recorded (the verifier recomputes the roots in $\mathbb{F}*{\ell^r}$, which are canonical up to the relabeling that $\pi$ absorbs: the verifier finds its own roots $\beta'$ and a permutation $\sigma$ with $\beta'=\beta^\sigma$ by matching residues of $f$'s factors; alternatively, the certificate records $\beta$ as elements of $\mathbb{F}*{\ell^r}$ given by a defining polynomial, $O(nr\log\ell)$ bits). *Check:* [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md): the counts by $\gcd$ computations on $R\_{C,\chi}\bmod\lambda$, the class by [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Thm. 4.2 to 5.1; [the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md): $F\_i(\beta^{\pi})\equiv c\_i\bmod\ell$ for all $i$ (and $\ell\notin E$), $\pi^{-1}\phi\_\beta\pi$ in the claimed class. *Justification:* [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Theorems 4.3, 5.1; [the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md) Theorem 3.1 (with [matching](https://claude.ai/chat/matching.md) Theorem 2.3 for the residue-field variant).

**U4 Frobenius class.** The class index $k(\ell)$. *Check:* consistency with U1 to U3 (cycle type, rational class, and, when present, the exact class). The classes recorded over all $\ell\le X$ must generate $G$ (used in G0.2).

## 3. Per character $\chi$

**C1 Conductor.** For each ramified $\ell$: the vector $N\_k=\sum\_{\sigma\in D\_0\cap C\_k,\sigma\ne1}i\_L(\sigma)$, $\delta(L)$, $f\_\ell(\chi)$; $\mathfrak{f}(\chi)$; $\mathfrak{f}(\det\rho\_\chi)$, $\mathrm{Stab}(\chi)$, the fields $N\_\lambda$ with their discriminants ([the global conductor](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md)). *Check:* $f\_\ell(\chi)=\frac{1}{|D\_0|}(\chi(1)\delta(L)-\sum\_kN\_k\chi(g\_k))\in\mathbb{Z}*{\ge0}$ from R3 and the table; $\sum*\chi\chi(1)f\_\ell(\chi)=[G\:D\_\ell]f,v\_L(m'(\varpi\_L))$ ([conductor exponents](https://claude.ai/chat/conductor-exponents.md) §4, the identity); $f\_\ell(\pi\_{H\_j})=v\_\ell(\mathrm{disc},\mathcal{O}\_{K\_j})$ against G0.5; [the global conductor](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md) checks I to IV. *Justification:* [conductor exponents](https://claude.ai/chat/conductor-exponents.md) Prop. 1.1, Theorems 2.3, 4.1; [the global conductor](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md) Theorems 3.1, 4.1, 5.1, 6.3.

**C2 Ramified Euler factors.** For each ramified $\ell$: $\dim V\_\chi^{I\_\ell}=\langle\chi|*{I*\ell},1\rangle$, the coset class counts of $\mathrm{Fr}^kI\_\ell$ ($k\<f$), the multiplicities $\langle\chi|*{D*\ell},\psi\_j\rangle$, and $P\_\ell(\chi;T)\in\mathcal{O}*{\mathbb{Q}(\chi)}[T]$; for the characters of family 3, the model computation (*[*Euler factors*](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md) *(A)) with the projector rank. Check: recompute $P*\ell$ by [Euler factors](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md) (C) from R3's class labels and the table; $\deg P=\dim V^I=\chi(1)-(f\_\ell(\chi)-\mathrm{sw}*\ell(\chi))$; $P\mid(1-T^f)^{\dim V^I}$; $\prod*\chi P\_\ell(\chi;T)^{\chi(1)}=(1-T^f)^{[G\:D\_\ell]}$; permutation characters against the residue degrees of G0.5; when the model is present, $\det(1-AT)=P^m$ and rank $e\_I=m\dim V^I$. *Justification:* [Euler factors](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md) Theorem 2.1, Prop. 3.1 and §3.

**C3 Unramified Euler factors up to $X$.** Not stored: derived from U4 and the eigenvalue multiplicities $m\_j(\mathrm{Fr}\_\ell)$ of [the character table](https://claude.ai/chat/character-table.md) Prop. 2.1(4). *Check:* recomputed as needed.

**C4 Root number.** $W(\chi)$ and its local factorization $(\varepsilon\_\infty,{\varepsilon\_\ell})$ with $a\_\chi,b\_\chi$, the class of $c$ with its real-root counts ([the archimedean place](https://claude.ai/chat/archimedean.md): $r$ for $f$ and $\mathrm{fix}*{G/H}(c)$ for the family), and per ramified $\ell$ the type and the R4 data; $\nu\_2(\chi)$ and the shortcut applied (*[*the global root number*](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) *§5) with its outcome. Check: $\chi(c)\equiv\chi(1)\bmod2$; real-root counts by Sturm; $\varepsilon*\infty=i^{-b\_\chi}$; product $=W$, $|W|=1$, $W(\bar\chi)=\overline{W(\chi)}$; the shortcut's prediction (quadratic: $W=1$; orthogonal: $W=1$ and the local ratios $\zeta\_{\rho,v}$ where [the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) Prop. 3.3 applies; symplectic: every local factor $\pm1$); Galois equivariance across conjugate characters. *Justification:* [the archimedean place](https://claude.ai/chat/archimedean.md) Prop. 1.1, 2.1, 4.1; [the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) Prop. 1.1, Cor. 3.2, Prop. 3.3, Prop. 4.1.

**C5 Functional-equation defect.** The list of test points $t\_1,\dots,t\_r$ (with $t=1$ excluded when $W=-1$ is under test), $X$, the certified kernel-evaluation precision, and for each $t$ the values $S\_\chi(t,X)$, $S\_{\bar\chi}(1/t,X)$, the defect $|\Delta\_\chi(t,X;W)|$ and the bound $\mathcal{E}(X,\chi,t)$; for self-dual $\chi$ with candidates $\pm1$, the defects for both candidates. *Check:* recompute the sums from C2 to C3 and the kernel; $|\Delta|\le\mathcal{E}$ at every $t\_i$ for the recorded $W$, and, where a sign was decided, $|\Delta(W')|>\mathcal{E}$ for the rejected candidate at some $t\_i$ with the margin of [the functional-equation test](https://claude.ai/chat/functional-equation.md) Thm. 4.1. *Justification:* [the functional-equation test](https://claude.ai/chat/functional-equation.md) Theorems 2.2, 4.1 and §5, this is a rejection test; see §5 below for what it does and does not establish.

**C6 Subfield identities.** For each $H\in\mathcal{H}$: $n\_\chi(H)$, $c\_m(H)$ for $m\le X$ (from the splitting data of $R\_H$, reused from U2), $d\_{F\_H}$, $(r\_1,r\_2)$, and the four identities of [subfield zeta identities](https://claude.ai/chat/subfield-zeta.md) §2 with their outcomes. *Check:* recompute both sides. *Justification:* [subfield zeta identities](https://claude.ai/chat/subfield-zeta.md) Theorem 1.1, Prop. 2.1.

## 4. Soundness

**Theorem 4.1.** Suppose $\mathsf{V}$ accepts $\mathcal{C}(f,X)$. Then, with respect to the numbering G0.1 and up to the conjugacy ambiguities of [well-definedness](https://claude.ai/chat/well-definedness.md):

1. $G$ is the Galois group of $f$ (G0.2 with U4).
2. The table G0.3 is the character table of $G$.
3. The set G0.5 is exactly the set of ramified primes, with the recorded local invariants.
4. For each ramified $\ell$: $D\_\ell$, $I\_\ell$, $(D\_\ell)\_i$, the Herbrand function, the upper breaks and the Frobenius coset are correct in both numberings (R1 to R3).
5. For each unramified $\ell\le X$: the recorded class of $\mathrm{Frob}\_\ell$ is correct (U1 to U4).
6. For each $\chi$: $\mathfrak{f}(\chi)$, all $f\_\ell(\chi)$, the Euler factors $P\_\ell(\chi;T)$ at every prime $\le X$ and at every ramified prime, $\gamma\_\chi$, and $W(\chi)$ with its local factors are correct (C1 to C4).
7. The functional equation $\Lambda(s,\chi)=W(\chi)\Lambda(1-s,\bar\chi)$ has been tested at the recorded points with no rejection, and for the self-dual characters whose sign was decided numerically the rejected candidate is excluded (C5); the subfield identities hold coefficientwise up to $X$ (C6).

*Proof.* Each check is the hypothesis of the theorem cited as its justification, and those theorems are unconditional; the invariance statements of [well-definedness](https://claude.ai/chat/well-definedness.md) remove the dependence on the choices; the tie to the numbering is G0.2 (the values $c\_i$) reproduced at every prime in R2 and U3, which by [matching](https://claude.ai/chat/matching.md) Theorem 2.3 and [subfield zeta identities](https://claude.ai/chat/subfield-zeta.md) Theorem 3.4 excludes a Galois relabeling of any prime's data relative to the $p$-numbering. $\square$

**What acceptance does not establish.** (a) Artin holomorphy: C5 is a rejection test ([the functional-equation test](https://claude.ai/chat/functional-equation.md) Remark 2.3); for family 3 and monomial $\chi$ holomorphy is a theorem and C5 is then a consistency check only. (b) Frobenius classes at $\ell>X$. (c) The root number of a symplectic $\chi$ beyond the numerical decision of C5 ([the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) §4); all other root numbers are proved by R4/C4. (d) Nothing about $N$ or its subfields beyond what the resolvents of degree $\le\max\_H[G\:H]$ define.

## 5. Size and verification cost

Global part: $O(n^2\log|G|)$ for the group data, $O(r^2\log(|G|))$ bits for the table with $|S|\cdot r^2$ integers for its certificate, $O(\sum\_i m\_i)$ for the chain. Per ramified prime: the tower and roots at precision $k\_\ell$, $O(n|D\_\ell|k\_\ell\log\ell)$ bits; the class labels $O(|D\_\ell|\log r)$; the filtration $O(r\_I)$ integers; the root-number data $O(\sum\_\chi(#\text{orbits}+#\text{Brauer terms}))$ algebraic numbers. Per unramified prime: $O(\log r+|\mathcal{H}*\lambda|)$ integers, plus $O(nr\log\ell)$ bits when U3(*[*the direct route*](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md)*) is used. Per character: $O(#\text{ramified})$ polynomials and integers, and $r$ pairs $(\Delta,\mathcal{E})$. Total: polynomial in $n,|G|,\log\Delta,\pi(X)$ and $\max|D*\ell|$; in particular it never contains an object of size comparable to $[N:\mathbb{Q}]=|G|$ beyond the local groups.

Verification cost is dominated by: (i) the Hensel lifts and invariant evaluations in the towers at ramified primes ($\tilde O(k\_\ell|D\_\ell|\sum\_im\_ic\_i\log\ell)$ each, [matching](https://claude.ai/chat/matching.md) §4, but only for the recorded $\tau'\_i$, not the whole transversal, so $\sum\_ic\_i$ evaluations); (ii) the factorizations of $f$ and of the resolvents modulo each $\ell\le X$ ($\tilde O(\pi(X)\sum\_H[G\:H]\log X)$); (iii) the coefficient assembly and the smoothed sums ($\tilde O(X)$ per character); (iv) the Gauss and Tate sums at ramified primes. The verifier performs no computation whose cost depends on a search: the transversals are scanned only at the recorded element, the Brauer decompositions are checked as identities of class functions, and the separating families are checked for separation by a single table computation.
