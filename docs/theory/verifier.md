# The verifier

**Input.** $f$, $X$, and the certificate $\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{C}(f,X)$ of [the certificate](https://claude.ai/chat/certificate.md). **Output.** ACCEPT or REJECT (with the first failing predicate). **Size conventions** (fixed here so that §4 holds): every class label of an element $\sigma\in G$ comes with a conjugator $g$ ($g\sigma g^{-1}=g\_k$); the BSGS of $G$ and of every recorded local group is part of $\mathcal{C}$; the roots used by [the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md)-type alignments are recorded as elements of $\mathbb{F}\_{\ell^r}$; the Gauss and Tate sums of [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md) to [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) are recorded together with the value tables of the residue characters on $(\mathcal{O}\_F/\mathfrak{p}^{\lceil a/2\rceil})^\times$ (size $q\_F^{\lceil a/2\rceil}$); all algebraic numbers are given exactly in the relevant cyclotomic field. $|\mathcal{C}|$ denotes the total bit size.

## 1. The predicates

$\mathsf{V}$ evaluates the following in order; each is a deterministic recomputation from $f$, $X$ and $\mathcal{C}$.

**V0 (group and table).** (a) The BSGS of $G$ is valid (Schreier to Sims verification) and the generators lie in $S\_n$. (b) The chain data: $\mathrm{Stab}*{G*{i-1}}(F\_i)=G\_i$; $F\_i(\hat\alpha)\equiv c\_i\bmod p^{k\_p}$; $\mathrm{Res}\_i$ reproduced at precision $k\_p$, $\mathrm{disc},\mathrm{Res}\_i\ne0$, $\mathrm{Res}\_i(c\_i)=0$. (c) The recorded generating Frobenius classes (U4) generate $G$ (closure of the subgroup generated by the class representatives is $G$). (d) The table: [the character table](https://claude.ai/chat/character-table.md) Prop. 2.2's identities $M\_i\omega=\omega\_i\omega$ for $i\in S$ with distinct tuples and $\omega\_1=1$; $\chi(g\_k)=\chi(1)\omega\_k/|C\_k|$ with $\chi(1)$ the positive square root of the recorded rational; power maps consistent with orders. (e) $\mathbf{M}$ recomputed from $\mathcal{F}$; every invariant appearing in $\mathcal{C}$ is in $\mathcal{F}$ with the correct $m$. (f) Ramified-prime witnesses ([the certificate](https://claude.ai/chat/certificate.md) G0.5): for each candidate prime the recorded verdict is recomputed. (g) Abelian data: the period identities of [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Prop. 1.3 at precision $k\_p$ and $\iota(p)=\pi(\mathrm{Frob}\_p)$.

**V1 (local fields).** For each ramified $\ell$: (a) $f\equiv\prod\_jg\_j\pmod{\ell^{\nu+1}}$ with $v\_\ell(\mathrm{Res}(g\_a,g\_b))<(\nu+1)/2$; (b) each tower step's polynomial $q$ is irreducible over its base ([the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) Lemma 3.3 applied to the recorded factorization data at precision $>v(\mathrm{disc},q)$: the verifier recomputes that $q$ has no factor of lower degree modulo that precision via the recorded $\ell$-maximal-order/Newton data, or accepts the recorded irreducibility witness of [local Galois groups](https://claude.ai/chat/local-galois-groups.md) Construction 2.1); (c) $[L:\mathbb{Q}*\ell]=\prod\deg q=|D|$; (d) $f(\beta\_i)\equiv0\bmod\ell^{k*\ell}$ in $L$ and $v(\beta\_i-\beta\_j)\le\nu/2$ for $i\ne j$; (e) each recorded generator $d\_k$ of $D$: its images of the tower generators are roots of the transported minimal polynomials at precision $k\_\ell$, and the induced map sends $\beta\_i$ to $\beta\_{d\_k(i)}$; (f) the BSGS of $\langle d\_k\rangle$ is valid with order $|D|$, $D$ is solvable, and its orbits on ${\beta\_i}$ are the root sets of the $g\_j$.

**V2 (matchings).** For each ramified $\ell$: (a) $F\_i(\beta^\rho)\equiv c\_i\pmod{\ell^{k\_\ell}}$ for $i=1,\dots,t$ (evaluation in $L$); (b) $\rho^{-1}d\_k\rho\in G$ for every generator $d\_k$ (sifting through the BSGS of $G$); (c) the recorded $D\_\ell$ equals $\rho^{-1}D\rho$ and the recorded class labels of its elements are verified by their conjugators.

**V3 (filtrations).** For each ramified $\ell$: (a) $\omega$ is Teichmüller ($\omega^{\ell^f}=\omega$, residue of degree $f$) and $I={\sigma:\sigma\omega=\omega}$, $\Phi={\sigma:\sigma\omega=\omega^\ell}$ as recorded; (b) $v\_L(\varpi\_L)=1$ and, for each recorded class representative $\sigma\in I$, $i\_L(\sigma)=v\_L(\sigma\varpi\_L-\varpi\_L)$ recomputed at precision $\nu+1$; (c) $D\_i={i\_L\ge i+1}$ are normal in $D$, $D\_1$ is the $\ell$-Sylow subgroup of $D\_0$, and the quotients $D\_i/D\_{i+1}$ ($i\ge1$) are elementary abelian $\ell$-groups; (d) Hilbert: $\sum\_{\sigma\ne1}i\_L(\sigma)=v\_L(m'(\varpi\_L))$ with $m$ the minimal polynomial of $\varpi\_L$ over $L^{\mathrm{ur}}$, recomputed; (e) [filtration uniqueness](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md) Check 1 (Hasse to Arf on every abelian $D\_a/D\_b$, from the orders) and Check 2 (orbit sizes of $I$ and $P$ on each $g\_j$ equal $e\_j$ and $\ell^{a\_j}$, with $f\_j$ orbits); (f) $\varphi\_L$, the upper breaks and $\delta(L)$ recomputed from the orders; (g) if [filtration uniqueness](https://claude.ai/chat/filtration-uniqueness.md) §2 data are present: $y\_S$ generates $\mathcal{O}*{L^S}$ (*[*the ramified primes*](https://claude.ai/chat/ramified-primes.md) *test at precision $\nu+1$), $R\_S$ is its minimal polynomial, the Newton polygon and labeled valuations are recomputed and agree with $|S\cap I|+i\_L(\sigma)-1$; (h) $v*\ell(d\_N)=[G\:D\_\ell]\cdot f\cdot v\_L(m'(\varpi\_L))$ as recorded.

**V4 (conductors).** For each ramified $\ell$ and each $\chi$: (a) $N\_k$ recomputed from the class labels of $D\_0\cap C\_k$ and the $i\_L$ values; (b) $f\_\ell(\chi)=\frac{1}{|D\_0|}\big(\chi(1)\delta(L)-\sum\_kN\_k\chi(g\_k)\big)$ is a nonnegative integer equal to the recorded value; (c) $\sum\_\chi\chi(1)f\_\ell(\chi)=v\_\ell(d\_N)$ of V3(h); (d) $f\_\ell(\pi\_{H\_j})=v\_\ell(\mathrm{disc},\mathcal{O}*{K\_j})$ from V0(f); (e)* [*the global conductor*](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md) *checks I to IV with $\det\rho*\chi$ recomputed by [the global conductor](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md) Prop. 2.1 and the discriminants of the fields $N\_\lambda$ from the recorded resolvents ([the ramified primes](https://claude.ai/chat/ramified-primes.md) recomputation).

**V5 (ramified Euler factors).** For each ramified $\ell$ and each $\chi$: (a) $\dim V^I=\langle\chi|*{I*\ell},1\rangle$ recomputed; (b) the coset class counts of $\mathrm{Fr}^kI\_\ell$ recomputed from the class labels; (c) $P\_\ell(\chi;T)=\prod\_j(1-\zeta\_f^jT)^{\langle\chi|*{D*\ell},\psi\_j\rangle}$ recomputed and equal to the recorded polynomial, with coefficients in $\mathcal{O}*{\mathbb{Q}(\chi)}$; (d) $\deg P=\dim V^I=\chi(1)-f*\ell(\chi)+\mathrm{sw}*\ell(\chi)$; (e) $\prod*\chi P\_\ell(\chi;T)^{\chi(1)}=(1-T^f)^{[G\:D\_\ell]}$ and the permutation-character factors equal $\prod\_\mathfrak{p}(1-T^{f\_\mathfrak{p}})$ from V0(f); (f) when a model computation is recorded: the projector rank and $\det(1-AT)=P^m$.

**V6 (Frobenius classes).** For each unramified $\ell\le X$: (a) the cycle type of $f\bmod\ell$ equals the recorded block and the block of the recorded class; (b) for each $H\in\mathcal{H}*\lambda$, the number of degree-one factors of $R\_H\bmod\ell$ (by $\deg\gcd(x^\ell-x,R\_H)$; $\ell$-adically at precision $k*\ell$ if $\ell\in E\_H$) equals $\pi\_H$ evaluated on the recorded rational class via [rational classes](https://claude.ai/chat/rational-classes.md) Lemma 1.1 from the table, and the family's signatures separate the block (one table computation per block, cached); (c) when an [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) refinement is recorded: $t=\ell\bmod M$, the twisted counts $n\_j$ recomputed by $\deg\gcd(R\_{C,\chi},x^{(N\lambda-1)/o}-\zeta\_o^j)$ over $\mathbb{F}*\lambda$, $\ell$ in the recorded exactness residue class, and the class of $\sigma*\ell^{f}$ and of $\sigma\_\ell$ deduced as in [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Theorems 4.3 to 5.1; when an [the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md) alignment is recorded: the recorded $\beta\in\mathbb{F}*{\ell^r}^n$ are the roots of $f$ ($f(\beta\_i)=0$, distinct), $F\_i(\beta^{\pi})=c\_i\bmod\ell$ for all $i$ with $\ell\notin E$, $\phi*\beta$ recomputed from $\beta\_i^\ell$, and $\pi^{-1}\phi\_\beta\pi$ lies in the recorded class (conjugator supplied); (d) the recorded class is consistent with (a) to (c).

**V7 (root numbers).** For each $\chi$: (a) $\chi(c)\equiv\chi(1)\bmod2$, $a\_\chi,b\_\chi$, $\varepsilon\_\infty=i^{-b\_\chi}$; the class of $c$ verified by V6-type resolvent real-root counts (Sturm) on the involution block; (b) tame $\ell$: $\varpi\_L^e=\omega\ell$; $\theta\_0(\sigma)=\sigma\varpi\_L/\varpi\_L$; $n\_\theta$ from the table; $\Phi\_\theta$'s two properties of [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md) Prop. 3.1(2); the Gauss sums recomputed from the recorded value tables; the determinants recomputed from the table; the product equals the recorded $\varepsilon\_\ell(\chi)$; (c) wild $\ell$: the identity $\mathrm{Res}*D\chi-\chi(1)\mathbf{1}=\sum c*{A,\mu}\mathrm{Ind}*A^D(\mu-\mathbf{1}A)$ as class functions on the class representatives of $D$; $a(\xi{A,\mu})$ and $d\_A$ from V3; the Lubin to Tate data: $*[*\pi^{n}*](https://claude.ai/chat/%5Clambda_n)*=0\ne*[*\pi^{n-1}*](https://claude.ai/chat/%5Clambda_n)*$, the recorded expression of the generator $y$ of $M$ in $\lambda\_n$ and a root of unity satisfies $y$'s minimal polynomial, and for each recorded $u$ the substitution $*[*u^{-1}*](https://claude.ai/chat/%5Clambda_n)*$ reproduces $\sigma\_u(y)$ for the recorded $\sigma\_u\in\mathrm{Gal}(M/F\_A)$; the Tate sums recomputed from the value tables; the product equals the recorded $\varepsilon*\ell(\chi)$; (d) $W(\chi)=\varepsilon\_\infty\prod\varepsilon\_\ell$, $|W|=1$, $W(\bar\chi)=\overline{W(\chi)}$; the [the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) §5 shortcut recorded for $\chi$ has the hypothesis it needs ($\nu\_2(\chi)$ recomputed) and its prediction is met, including the local ratios $\zeta\_{\rho,v}$ where [the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) Prop. 3.3 applies.

**V8 (identities).** (a) [the Euler identities](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-identities.md): for each $H\in\mathcal{H}$ and each ramified $\ell$, $\prod\_\chi P\_\ell(\chi;T)^{\langle\chi|*H,1\rangle}=Z*{H,\ell}(T)$ with $Z\_{H,\ell}$ from the recorded splitting of $R\_H$ (V0(f)/V6(b) data); (b) [subfield zeta identities](https://claude.ai/chat/subfield-zeta.md): for each $H\in\mathcal{H}$, $c\_m(H)=b\_m(H)$ for $m\le X$ (Dirichlet convolution of the recomputed coefficient sequences), $|d\_{F\_H}|=\prod\mathfrak{f}(\chi)^{n\_\chi(H)}$, $r\_1(H)=\sum n\_\chi(H)\chi(c)$ against the Sturm count of $R\_H$, $\prod W(\chi)^{n\_\chi(H)}=1$.

**V9 (defects).** For each $\chi$ and each recorded $t$: recompute $S\_\chi(t,X)$, $S\_{\bar\chi}(1/t,X)$ with kernel values certified to the recorded precision, $\mathcal{E}(X,\chi,t)$ by [the functional-equation test](https://claude.ai/chat/functional-equation.md) Prop. 3.1, and check $|\Delta\_\chi(t,X;W)|\le\mathcal{E}$; where a sign was decided, $|\Delta(W')|>\mathcal{E}$ at some recorded $t$ for the rejected $W'$.

$\mathsf{V}$ accepts iff all predicates hold.

## 2. Soundness for conductors and ramified Euler factors

**Hypothesis (G).** The certificate for $G$ is correct: $G$ is the Galois group of $f$ acting on the numbering $\hat\alpha$ (this is what V0(b) to (c) establish, given the theorems of [the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) and [rational classes](https://claude.ai/chat/rational-classes.md): V0(b) proves $\mathrm{Gal}\le G$, and V0(c) with V6 proves $\mathrm{Gal}\supseteq G$, because each certified Frobenius class is the class in $G$ of an element of $\mathrm{Gal}$, [rational classes](https://claude.ai/chat/rational-classes.md) Prop. 1.2 needs only $\mathrm{Gal}\le G$, and these generate $G$).

**Theorem 2.1.** Assume (G) and that $\mathsf{V}$ accepts. Then for every ramified $\ell$ and every $\chi\in\mathrm{Irr}(G)$, the recorded $f\_\ell(\chi)$ is the Artin conductor exponent and the recorded $P\_\ell(\chi;T)$ is the Euler factor $\det(1-\rho\_\chi(\mathrm{Fr}*\ell)T\mid V*\chi^{I\_\ell})$; hence $\mathfrak{f}(\chi)$ and the ramified part of $L(s,\chi)$ are correct. The proof uses only V0(d), V1 to V5 and the theorems of [the character table](https://claude.ai/chat/character-table.md), [the precision policy](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md), [local Galois groups](https://claude.ai/chat/local-galois-groups.md) to [Euler factors](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md); it does not use V6 to V9, V0(f) to (g), nor the induction data of [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md) to [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md).

*Proof.* Fix $\ell$. V1(b) to (c): the tower is a field $L$ of degree $|D|$ over $\mathbb{Q}*\ell$; V1(d): the $\beta\_i$ are $n$ distinct approximate roots, and by* [*the precision policy*](https://claude.ai/chat/precision-policy.md) *Lemma 3.3 /* [*local Galois groups*](https://claude.ai/chat/local-galois-groups.md) *Prop. 1.1 each is congruent modulo $\ell^{k*\ell}$ to a unique true root of $f$ in $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}*\ell$, the true roots lie in $L$, and $L$ is the splitting field of $f$ over $\mathbb{Q}*\ell$ once they generate it, which V1(f) (orbits are the factors) and V1(c) together with the recorded tower (each step adjoining a root) guarantee. V1(e) to (f): the $d\_k$ are automorphisms of $L$ over $\mathbb{Q}*\ell$ and generate a group of order $[L:\mathbb{Q}*\ell]$, hence all of $\mathrm{Gal}(L/\mathbb{Q}*\ell)$; so the recorded $D$ is $\mathrm{Gal}(f/\mathbb{Q}*\ell)$ acting on the numbering $\beta$ ([local Galois groups](https://claude.ai/chat/local-galois-groups.md) Theorem 4.1). V2(a) to (b): by [matching](https://claude.ai/chat/matching.md) Theorem 2.3 (whose hypotheses are (G), the chain certificate V0(b), and the precision $k\_\ell$ of V0(e)), $\rho^{-1}$ is a matching, so $\rho^{-1}D\rho=D\_\ell$ is the decomposition group of a prime above $\ell$ in the global numbering, well defined up to $G$-conjugacy ([well-definedness](https://claude.ai/chat/well-definedness.md)). V2(c): the class labels are verified by conjugators, so all sums over classes below are correct. V3(a): [the filtration](https://claude.ai/chat/ramification-filtration.md) Prop. 1.1 identifies $I$ and $\Phi$; V3(b): $\varpi\_L$ is a uniformizer and $i\_L(\sigma)=v\_L(\sigma\varpi\_L-\varpi\_L)$ is exact at precision $\nu+1$ by [the filtration](https://claude.ai/chat/ramification-filtration.md) Prop. 2.2 and Lemma 2.3, for the recorded representatives, hence for all of $I$ by conjugation invariance and V2(c); V3(c) confirms the chain is the one defined by $i\_L$. V4(a) to (b): [conductor exponents](https://claude.ai/chat/conductor-exponents.md) Prop. 1.1 with the verified $i\_L$, class labels and table (V0(d)) gives $f\_\ell(\chi)$; the recorded value equals the recomputed one. V5(a) to (c): [Euler factors](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md) Theorem 2.1 (C) with the verified coset labels gives $P\_\ell(\chi;T)$. The remaining predicates in V3 to V5 (Hilbert, Hasse to Arf, orbits, the [conductor exponents](https://claude.ai/chat/conductor-exponents.md) identity, [the global conductor](https://claude.ai/chat/global-conductor.md), degree and divisibility, zeta identity, the model) are consequences of the above under (G) and serve to localize errors; they are not needed for the conclusion. $\square$

**Corollary 2.2.** Under the same hypotheses: the filtration $(D\_\ell)*i$, its Herbrand function and upper breaks, $v*\ell(d\_N)$ (V3(h) with V3(d)), $\dim V\_\chi^{I\_\ell}$, $\mathrm{sw}\_\ell(\chi)$, and every quantity of [the filtration](https://claude.ai/chat/ramification-filtration.md) to [Euler factors](https://claude.ai/chat/euler-factors.md) that is a conjugation-invariant function of the filtered datum with its Frobenius coset are correct; and, adding V0(g) and V6, every recorded Frobenius class at $\ell\le X$ is correct ([rational classes](https://claude.ai/chat/rational-classes.md) Theorem 4.1, [cyclotomic refinement](https://claude.ai/chat/cyclotomic-refinement.md) Theorems 4.3/5.1, [the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md) Theorem 3.1), hence every unramified Euler factor up to $X$.

## 3. Outputs that depend on the induction data

The wild local root numbers rest on [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md): a Brauer decomposition $\theta=\sum c\_{A,\mu}\mathrm{Ind}\_A^D(\mu-\mathbf{1}*A)$ and reciprocity values $\xi*{A,\mu}$ obtained through Lubin to Tate theory. The decomposition itself is checked as an identity of class functions (V7(c)) and any decomposition gives the same value (Deligne's uniqueness), so the dependence is not on the *choice* of induction data but on their *reciprocity content*.

**Theorem 3.1.** Assume (G) and acceptance.

1. In *full mode* (the certificate contains the Lubin to Tate data and $\mathsf{V}$ runs V7(c) entirely): the wild $\varepsilon\_\ell(\chi)$ are correct ([wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) Theorem 3.1 with Prop. 2.3, every input of which is recomputed), hence all $W(\chi)$ are correct ([the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md) Prop. 1.1 with V7(a),(b),(d)).
2. In *light mode* (the Lubin to Tate embedding is not recomputed, only the decomposition identity, the conductor exponents, the value tables' consistency $|G|^2=q^a$ and the Tate sums): the recorded $\xi\_{A,\mu}$ are trusted, and the following outputs carry this additional dependence: $\varepsilon\_\ell(\chi)$ at every wildly ramified $\ell$ with $\chi|*{(D*\ell)*1}\ne\chi(1)\mathbf{1}$; $W(\chi)$ for every such $\chi$; the sign decisions and defects of V9 and the identity $\prod*\chi W(\chi)^{n\_\chi(H)}=1$ of V8(b) for those $\chi$. For these, acceptance establishes correctness only up to a root of unity at each such prime, and the analytic test V9 together with [the global root number](https://claude.ai/chat/global-root-numbers.md)'s shortcuts (quadratic, orthogonal, whose predictions do not depend on [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md), and the reality constraint for symplectic $\chi$) is the remaining evidence.
3. Conductors, all Euler factors, Gamma factors, tame root numbers, archimedean root numbers, and $W(\chi)$ for every $\chi$ unramified at all wild primes or trivial on all wild inertia groups do not depend on [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) in either mode.

*Proof.* 1: every quantity entering [wild root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/wild-root-numbers.md) Theorem 3.1 is recomputed by V7(c). 2: only the reciprocity values are taken from the certificate; a wrong value changes $\varepsilon(\xi\_{A,\mu},\psi\_{F\_A})$ by a factor $\xi'(c)/\xi(c)$ and by a permuted Gauss-type sum of the same modulus, so the error is a unit complex number; the listed outputs are exactly those in which wild $\varepsilon\_\ell$ enter. 3: Theorem 2.1, [the archimedean place](https://claude.ai/chat/archimedean.md), [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md), and the fact that $\varepsilon\_\ell(\chi)=\varepsilon\_\ell(\chi|*{D*\ell})$ involves only the tame theory when $(D\_\ell)*1$ acts trivially on $V*\chi$ (then $\chi|*{D*\ell}$ factors through the tame quotient $D\_\ell/(D\_\ell)\_1$ and [tame root numbers](https://claude.ai/chat/tame-root-numbers.md) applies to it). $\square$

## 4. Verification is polynomial in the certificate size

**Theorem 4.1.** With the size conventions of the header, $\mathsf{V}$ runs in time polynomial in $|\mathcal{C}|$ (and in $\log X$, $\log\Delta$, which are bounded by $|\mathcal{C}|$).

*Proof.* Each predicate is one of the following operations, each polynomial in its input size, applied polynomially many times:

- Permutation-group operations with a given BSGS: sifting (membership), products, Schreier to Sims verification, orbit computation, subgroup closure, solvability test, conjugacy *verification* by a supplied conjugator: all polynomial in $n$ and the number of generators (V0(a),(c); V1(f); V2(b),(c); V3(c),(e); V6(c),(d)). No conjugacy *search* is performed, by the conjugator convention.
- Exact linear algebra over $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta\_e]$ of dimension $r\le|G|$ with $|S|$ matrices (V0(d)): $O(|S|r^3)$ ring operations on integers of size polynomial in $|\mathcal{C}|$.
- Arithmetic in $\mathcal{O}*q/p^{k\_p}$ and in the towers $\mathcal{O}L/\ell^{k\ell}$: evaluation of the straight-line programs of the $F\_i$ ($\sum\_ic\_i$ operations), of $f$, of minimal polynomials and of automorphism images (V0(b), V1(d),(e), V2(a), V3(b),(d),(g), V7(b),(c)); each operation costs polynomial in $|D*\ell|k\_\ell\log\ell\le|\mathcal{C}|$, and the number of operations is bounded by the number of recorded objects.
- The $\ell$-maximal order test (V0(f), V3(g)): one radical and one multiplier-ring computation, polynomial by [the ramified primes](https://claude.ai/chat/ramified-primes.md) Prop. 2.4.
- Factorization-type computations over $\mathbb{F}*\ell$: $\deg\gcd(x^\ell-x,R)$ and distinct-degree factorization by repeated squaring, polynomial in $\deg R$ and $\log\ell$ (V6(a),(b), V8); over $\mathbb{F}*\lambda$ likewise (V6(c)); $\ell$-adic root counts at precision $k\_\ell$ by Hensel from residue roots, polynomial (V6(b) at excluded primes).
- Evaluations in $\mathbb{F}\_{\ell^r}$ of recorded roots and invariants (V6(c), [the direct route](https://claude.ai/chat/direct-route.md) case): polynomial in $nr\log\ell$ and $\sum c\_i$; no root-finding, by the convention that $\beta$ is recorded.
- Sturm sequences on polynomials of degree $\le\max\_H[G\:H]$ with coefficients of size $\log|R\_H|$ (V7(a), V8(b)): polynomial.
- Gauss and Tate sums from recorded value tables (V7(b),(c)): linear in the table size $q\_F^{\lceil a/2\rceil}$, which is part of $|\mathcal{C}|$ by convention; Lubin to Tate checks: evaluation of $[\pi^n]$ and $[u^{-1}]$ on a recorded point in a field of recorded degree, polynomial.
- Class-function identities on class representatives (V7(c)), inner products with the table (V4, V5, V8): $O(r\cdot#\text{terms})$ ring operations.
- Polynomial arithmetic of degree $\le|G|$ (V5(e), V8(a)) and Dirichlet convolutions to $X$ (V8(b), V9): $O(X\log X)$ operations per factor, with $X\le|\mathcal{C}|$ since every prime up to $X$ has a record.
- Certified evaluation of $g\_\chi$ (closed forms or Mellin integrals) to the recorded precision and of the incomplete Gamma bounds (V9): polynomial in the precision and in $X$.

The number of predicates is linear in the number of recorded primes, characters and subgroups, so the total is polynomial in $|\mathcal{C}|$. $\square$

**Remark 4.2 (why the conventions are necessary).** Without conjugators, class identification is a conjugacy search in a permutation group, not known to be polynomial; without recorded roots in $\mathbb{F}\_{\ell^r}$, root-finding needs randomization or a factor of $\ell$; without the value tables, an exact Gauss sum over $k\_F$ costs $q\_F=\ell^{f}$ operations, exponential in the $O(f\log\ell)$ bits that describe $k\_F$; without the BSGS, $|G|$ and membership need Schreier to Sims from scratch, which is polynomial but not in terms of the certificate alone. Each convention adds to $|\mathcal{C}|$ exactly the data that make the corresponding check a recomputation rather than a search, which is the design principle of [the certificate](https://claude.ai/chat/certificate.md).
